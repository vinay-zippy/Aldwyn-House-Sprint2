ALDWYN HOUSE — HOTEL POLICIES

Check-in and Check-out
- Standard check-in time is 2:00 PM.
- Standard check-out time is 11:00 AM.
- Early check-in is subject to room availability.
- Late check-out may be requested through the Front Desk and is subject to availability.

Spa
- The Aldwyn House Spa is open daily from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
- Spa treatments should be reserved in advance.
- Guests should arrive at least 10 minutes before their scheduled treatment.

Restaurant
- The main restaurant is open daily from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM.
- Breakfast is served from 7:00 AM to 10:30 AM.
- Dinner service runs from 6:00 PM to 10:30 PM.
- Guests should contact the Front Desk for restaurant reservations.

Pets
- Pets are not permitted in the main restaurant.
- Service animals are permitted in accordance with applicable regulations.
- Guests should contact the Front Desk regarding the hotel's pet accommodation policy.

Pool
- The swimming pool is open daily from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
- Guests are requested to follow posted pool safety rules.
- Children must be supervised by an accompanying adult.

Gym
- The fitness centre is open 24 hours a day for hotel guests.
- Guests should use the equipment according to the posted safety instructions.

General
- Guests can contact the Front Desk for assistance with hotel services and requests.
- Hotel services and operating hours may be subject to change.
